<p><b>Lutrin</b> is a free and open-source PDF reader for Android: fast and private.</p>
<ul>
<li>No ads, no trackers, no telemetry.</li>
<li>No network permission: everything works offline, on your device.</li>
<li>Sharp rendering at any zoom level, continuous or page-by-page scrolling.</li>
<li>Table of contents, search, text selection and copy, links.</li>
<li>Password-protected PDFs (the password is never stored).</li>
<li>Recent documents and resume reading.</li>
<li>Light and dark themes, night and sepia document modes, full screen.</li>
</ul>
